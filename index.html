📖 Rank Budget Explained

Each team's FIFA world ranking position counts toward your budget. Rank #1 = 1 pt, Rank #48 = 48 pts. Your 5 picks must total ≥ 80. This prevents everyone from stacking the top 5 teams — you'll need to include at least some mid-tier or underdog nations to qualify.

Example: France(1) + Spain(2) + Argentina(3) + England(4) + Brazil(5) = 15 ❌ (too stacked). France(1) + Morocco(8) + Mexico(15) + Algeria(25) + Scotland(31) = 80 ✅
